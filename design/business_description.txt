AREABAZAAR BUSINESS DESCRIPTION  GENERAL DESCRIPTION OF BUSINESS SYSTEM  BRAND NAME : AreaBazaar BUSINESS TYPE : Ecommerce OPERATIONS DESCRIPTION : The business operates with a web application built with HTML,CSS(with Bootstrap) and Javascript (with Jquery) on the frontend, while the backend is a REST API Backend built with PHP/MySql (following a modified MVC structure) This web application is extensively integrated with the Google Workspace, especially to provide working interfaces for the Warehouse Managers and for the operational partnersâ€™ uses. Regional Area Agents (FRM) - who are responsible for publicizing the brand, publicizing information about impending offers and programs, facilitating payment by customers to deposit in their wallets, facilitating feedback from customers, facilitating pickup of bought items by customers daily ( , after the packages for customers in the region covered by the Area Agent has been dropped by the Zonal Bulk Dispatch team ) , attending to customers in the region for inquiries and requests for products and other miscellaneous functions. Regional Area Agents have unique Ref-codes which they include in their posted publicity materials , so that customers can see the Ref-code and include it when trying to register for a customer/user account.  These Ref-codes are used to track the customers that the FRM can be credited for when awarding commissions. Warehouse Manager (WHM) - who with their team facilitate the acquisition and handling of products purchased on a daily basis, including - quality check, splitting (when necessary), packaging, labelling and loading for transport . The WHM also carries out updates on product inventory that falls in the Warehouse Zone that is under their jurisdiction, as well as capture and addition of new products into the Product List/ Register. Products are located at one or more Warehouse Zones, where products bought on a daily basis are retrieved from the known or newly added Source Business Associates (SBA), then prepared and dispatched to the Regions of Operation , where Regional Area Agents are waiting to receive the batches of product Packages for onward delivery to the buyers. Customers can buy products that are displayed on the shop pages and confirm their orders by payment made from their wallet balance only. Customers can deposit funds in the wallet anytime, by requesting to do so on the website, after which they are provided with a Payment Reference Code to include in the narration when doing the fund transfer and the exact amount to be paid into the designated account. After making the payment / transfer, the customer proceeds to report the payment made, also specifying the Payment Reference Code used, so that the payment being reported can be easily and quickly traced by the Admin Operator, (or the system) , when attempting to confirm the payment has been made and therefore crediting the customer's wallet. The Customer may also choose to make the payment through a Regional Area Agent, who also follows a similar path for facilitating the payment for the customer. The customer however will need to Acknowledge that the crediting of their wallet has been effected, through an interface on the website, which should require entering their secret authentication details. The special shop pages called Auctions are made available for customers to purchase from within a designated start and stop time on the designated days. The designated Auction times are announced ahead of the times, on both the website and company social media. THE MANAGEMENT BACKEND The interfaces for the WHM are implemented in Google Workspace applications, mainly Sheets, by projecting the data to and from the Google Workspace, by leveraging the Google REST API, Google Appscript and the REST API routes prepared in the companyâ€™s PHP/MySql application backend The Split products section features bulk products that have been split into price-based units, based on the calculated equivalent quantities of products matching those price-units. The component of the system calculating those units, is to be operated by the WHM, by entering the necessary details needed for the calculation. When orders are received on a daily basis, the WHM component of the system has an interface to resolve all the products bought , - summing up all the total quantities of the different types of products bought, to facilitate the bulk acquisition of each product type. The other interfaces will be used to enter updates on each order item as they are being prepred, packed and labelled. Other components of the WHM section of the system should also help in preparing the labels for each order item to be prepared for dispatch. Wallet deposits exceeding a specified maximum, must NOT be automatically verified/confirmed by the system, without input by a Senior Admin Operator. The Admin operators can view and manage all that the WHM and the FRM enter into the system, and need to approve most of such entries before they are accepted for further processing by the system .
